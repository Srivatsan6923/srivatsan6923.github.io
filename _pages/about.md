<div class="page__about reveal" style="max-width: 820px; margin: 0 auto; line-height: 1.7;">
  <p>
    I am a <strong>Master's student</strong> in <strong>Electrical &amp; Computer Engineering (Machine Learning &amp; Data Science)</strong>
    at the <a href="https://ucsd.edu/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">University of California, San Diego</a>.
    Previously, I earned my B.Tech in Electronics &amp; Communication Engineering from
    <a href="https://www.nitt.edu/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">NIT Tiruchirappalli</a>,
    including a final-year exchange at the
    <a href="https://dsai.iitm.ac.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Wadhwani School of Data Science &amp; AI, IIT Madras</a>.
  </p>
  <p>
    My research spans multiple labs and institutions. At <strong>IIT Madras</strong> I work on 2D-to-3D coronary artery
    reconstruction from X-ray angiograms, an agentic AI framework for chest X-ray analysis, and frame-level CVS prediction
    for laparoscopic cholecystectomy. I previously worked on retinal OCTA microvascular &amp; FAZ segmentation and multimodal
    Alzheimer's classification at the <strong>MICAIH Lab, NIT Trichy</strong>, and ML-driven flexible antenna design on PDMS
    for sub-6&nbsp;GHz 5G wearables at the <strong>Motorola Lab, NIT Trichy</strong>. I've also interned at
    <strong>A*STAR SimTech, Singapore</strong> (spatter-defect segmentation for X-ray CT) and the
    <strong>Movement Control Lab, IISc Bengaluru</strong> (sEMG motor-unit recruitment analysis).
  </p>
  <p>
    <strong>Research interests:</strong> Computer Vision, Multimodal Learning, and Medical Image Analysis &mdash; with a focus on
    Agentic AI, Large Language Models, Generative Models, and Trustworthy &amp; Explainable AI for healthcare. I aim to build
    robust, interpretable systems that assist clinicians and improve care through human-centric, safe deployment.
  </p>
  <p>
    For collaborations, reach me at <a href="mailto:srivatsan6923@gmail.com">srivatsan6923@gmail.com</a>
    or <a href="mailto:ssarvesan@ucsd.edu">ssarvesan@ucsd.edu</a>.
  </p>
</div>
