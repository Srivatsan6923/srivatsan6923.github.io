A flexible hexagonal patch antenna on PDMS for sub-6 GHz 5G wearable applications,
achieving 3.2 dB peak gain with a validated low-cost fabrication process. Random Forest,
XGBoost, CatBoost, and LightGBM were compared to optimize antenna design parameters from
full-wave EM simulation data.
