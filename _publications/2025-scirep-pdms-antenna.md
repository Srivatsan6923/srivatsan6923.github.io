A flexible hexagonal patch antenna on PDMS for sub-6 GHz 5G wearable applications, paired
with a validated low-cost fabrication process. Random Forest, XGBoost, CatBoost, and
LightGBM were compared to optimize antenna design parameters from full-wave EM simulation data.
